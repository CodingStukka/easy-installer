Software Center, .deb, AppImage, .zip: what all these files are, which one you've got, and how to get it into your app menu.

App types on Linux

Which file did you download?

Linux apps come in a few different shapes. Here's what each one is, and what installs it.

  • Software Center

    App store: Snap and Flatpak

    Open the Software Center, search, click Install. The easiest way, if your app is there.

    INSTALLED WITHYour Software Center
  • .deb.rpm

    Packages

    Made for one family of systems: .deb for Ubuntu, Mint and Debian, .rpm for Fedora. Your system installs and updates them.

    INSTALLED WITHYour system
  • .AppImage

    AppImage

    One file with the whole app inside. Runs on almost any Linux, but on its own it isn't installed: no menu entry, no icon, no updates.

    INSTALLED WITHEasy Installer
  • .zip.tar.gz.tar.xz

    Portable archive

    A packed folder with the app inside. Unpack it and start the program. Again: no menu entry, nothing to update or remove it with.

    INSTALLED WITHEasy Installer
  • install.sh.run

    Installer script

    The maker's own installer. Follow their instructions. Easy Installer tells you when you've picked up one of these.

    INSTALLED WITHThe maker's instructions

.deb or AppImage?

Both are fine. They're just good at different things.

An AppImage is a portable app: one file that contains the whole program. No setup, nothing installed into your system. A .deb is a package your system installs and looks after.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

How do I install an AppImage on Linux?

Double-click the .AppImage file, or drag it onto Easy Installer. Check the name and icon, click Install, and the app appears in your app menu and search.

How do I install programs on Linux?

Look in your Software Center first. If the maker offers a .deb, double-click it. If you only get an AppImage or a .zip / .tar.gz, Easy Installer installs it for you.

Does Easy Installer make AppImages?

No. It doesn't build or change apps. It manages the AppImages and portable apps you download: menu entry, icon, updates and uninstalling.

Where are my installed apps stored?

In ~/Applications when you install just for yourself, or in /opt/appimages for everyone. The app's details in Easy Installer show the exact folder.

How do I uninstall an AppImage?

Click the menu next to the app in Easy Installer and choose Uninstall…, or right-click the app in your dock or app grid. Its data is only moved to the trash if you tick it.

Which Linux does it run on?

Ubuntu 24.04 or newer and systems based on it, like Zorin OS 18. It needs GTK 4.14 and libadwaita 1.5 or newer.
