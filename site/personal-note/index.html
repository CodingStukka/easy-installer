A personal note

Not a fight. Just an alternative.

Nobody should feel stuck with an operating system.

I believe a lot of people would switch in a heartbeat, if it didn't feel so different. I get that, and many will stay on Windows. That's fine. But Linux has become a real alternative: friendly distros made for beginners, and so many games that simply run now. Nobody should feel stuck.

Much of Linux is built by developers, for developers: often great, but missing that last bit of polish. With AI changing how software gets made, there's finally room for it. That's what I want to build. Not a fight against Apple or Microsoft, just an easy way in: feel at home, make the system your own, and maybe one day go from a beginner distro all the way to Arch.

— Stukka
